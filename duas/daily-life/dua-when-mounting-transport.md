# Dua When Mounting a Means of Transport

## Arabic

سُبْحَانَ الَّذِي سَخَّرَ لَنَا هَذَا وَمَا كُنَّا لَهُ مُقْرِنِينَ وَإِنَّا إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا لَمُنقَلِبُونَ

## Transliteration

Subhanal-ladhi sakhkhara lana hadha wa ma kunna lahu muqrinin, wa inna ila Rabbina lamunqalibun

## Translation

Glory be to Him who has subjected this to us, for we could never have accomplished it by ourselves. And indeed, to our Lord we will surely return.

## Reference

The words are from the Qur'an, Surah az-Zukhruf (43:13-14). The Prophet ﷺ would recite this upon mounting his riding animal for a journey, as reported by Muslim (1342) from Ibn Umar, often preceded by "Bismillah" and takbir three times.

## Notes

A dua I have long kept close for every journey — a reminder that no means of travel moves except by Allah's enabling, and that every journey ends in return to Him.
