# Dua of Gratitude — for Allah's Favours on Me and My Parents

## Arabic

رَبِّ أَوْزِعْنِىٓ أَنْ أَشْكُرَ نِعْمَتَكَ ٱلَّتِىٓ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَىَّ وَعَلَىٰ وَٰلِدَىَّ وَأَنْ أَعْمَلَ صَـٰلِحًا تَرْضَىٰهُ وَأَدْخِلْنِى بِرَحْمَتِكَ فِى عِبَادِكَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ

## Transliteration

Rabbi awzi'ni an ashkura ni'matakal-lati an'amta 'alayya wa 'ala walidayya wa an a'mala salihan tardahu wa adkhilni birahmatika fi 'ibadikas-salihin

## Translation

My Lord, inspire me to always be thankful for Your favours which You have blessed me and my parents with, and to do good deeds that please You. Admit me, by Your mercy, into the company of Your righteous servants.

## Reference

Qur'an, Surah an-Naml (27:19). The supplication of Prophet Sulayman (peace be upon him) upon smiling at the speech of the ant.

## Notes

A dua I keep returning to — it ties gratitude for one's own blessings to those given to one's parents, then asks for righteous deeds and the company of the righteous. A complete arc of thankfulness.
