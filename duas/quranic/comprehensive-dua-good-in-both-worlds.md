# The Comprehensive Dua — Good in Both Worlds

## Arabic

رَبَّنَآ ءَاتِنَا فِى ٱلدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِى ٱلْـَٔاخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ ٱلنَّارِ

## Transliteration

Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanatan wa fil-akhirati hasanatan wa qina 'adhaban-nar

## Translation

Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire.

## Reference

Qur'an, Surah al-Baqarah (2:201). The Prophet ﷺ frequently made this dua; Anas ibn Malik reported that the supplication the Prophet ﷺ most often said was this one (Bukhari 6389, Muslim 2690).

## Notes

The most comprehensive of duas — it asks for every good of this life and the next, and refuge from the greatest harm. Recommended between the two corners during tawaf.
